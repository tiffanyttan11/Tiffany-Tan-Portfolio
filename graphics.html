Branding · Visual Identity · Freelance

Fresh 汁

The brief

Describe who the client was and what they needed — a brand that felt modern and culturally grounded. What was the tension they were navigating, and what did they ask you to solve?

Direction

Growing up between China and the US, you know what it feels like when cultural references get flattened. Describe how that personal experience shaped the creative direction — holding both sides without choosing one.

Logo development

Walk through how the logo came together — explorations, what you tried, what you rejected, what principle guided the final mark.

Brand system

Describe the full visual language — color palette, typography, graphic elements, tone. How do the pieces work together?

Application

Show the identity in use — social media templates, packaging, signage, whatever you designed. This is where the system proves itself.

02

Product · Scent · Form study

Essence & Ember

The prompt

Design an object around a sensory experience. Open-ended — which made it harder, not easier. Write about where you started and why that openness was the real challenge.

Every decision was about removing, not adding — what does the form need to say, and what should it leave alone?

Exploration

Walk through your early formal explorations — how scent influenced form, what you were trying to express, what you edited out and why.

Final design

Describe the finished object. What does it feel like to hold? What does the form communicate before the function is even understood?
