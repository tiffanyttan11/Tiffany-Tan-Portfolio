About

Tiffany Tan —
Tingting.

In short

I create intentional and joyful experiences.

Born in China, raised in the US — I've always lived between two worlds, and that has allowed me to be adaptive and quick to understand differences easily.

I grew up drawing and crafting for most of my life. Making furniture, items for my plastic dolls and drawing realistic portraits of people. Discovering Industrial design allows me to expand and create meaningful products for people. Now I'm pursuing a BSD at ASU with a 3.9 GPA, designing across product, furniture, and branding.

My process always starts with research. Really understand the purpose and user before jumping into a situation.

I also speak conversational Mandarin and bring a bicultural lens to briefs that deal with cross-cultural experience or emotional nuance.
