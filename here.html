here

A screenless clip-on audio device for bedtime. Designed for Gen Z users who want to disconnect — no notifications, no light, just sound.

Year2026 – 2026
RoleSolo designer
ToolsSolidWorks · KeyShot · Figma
TypeAcademic / Concept

The problem

People reach for their phone at night not because they want to — because there's no satisfying alternative.

Insight

What starts as 30 minutes becomes hours of passive consumption before sleep.

Concept exploration

Started with the key experience of a screenless wearable, tactile buttons, fits comfortable in the palms of the hand etc.

Design decisions

I tested all of these prototypes with users, and reportedly 87% liked the rectangular shape was they were able to distinguished the different sides with functions.

Final design

1.7in x 1.7in x 0.64in with a back clip.
