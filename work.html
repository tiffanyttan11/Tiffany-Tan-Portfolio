02 / 05

Product · Wearable · UX

here

A screenless clip-on audio device designed for Gen Z users who want to wind down without a screen. The insight came from user research: people reach for their phone at night not because they want to — because there's no satisfying alternative.

here removes that friction. No display, no notifications — just a physical object that plays sound and disappears into the nightstand. Research-led from interviews through hardware exploration, CAD, and final renders.

03 / 05

Furniture · Material · Concept

Moni

A furniture-grade cat tree that doesn't look like one. The brief was self-initiated: why do objects designed for pets feel like they don't belong in the same room as human things?

Moni answers that with structural clarity and material honesty — the same principles that make a good bookshelf good. Warmth without decoration. Presence without apology.

Fresh 汁

Full brand identity for a small business — logo design, visual language, and a social media system that scales. The client wanted something that felt both modern and culturally rooted.

Growing up between China and the US, I know what it feels like when cultural references get flattened. This project was an exercise in holding both sides without choosing one — letting the tension be the identity.

05 / 05

Product · Scent · Form study

Essence & Ember

A concept exploration in form language and sensory design. The prompt was open — design an object around a sensory experience — which made it harder, not easier.

The result is an exercise in restraint. Every decision was about removing, not adding: what does the form need to say, and what should it leave alone?
