My Med

Your travel medication guide, every step of the way.

12 weeks
Figma, Photoshop, Keyshot
Team of 4
UX Research, UI Design, Prototyping

Overview

Medication regulations vary by country, carrier, and border crossing and there's no single place to find them.

Travelers check pharmacists, insurance providers, embassy websites, and Google The information exists, it's just scattered everywhere.

We built the guide that doesn't exist yet.

Define

Understanding current situation

Started with researching, analyzing current healthcare interface experiences through literature, market analysis, and user interviews.

Research

Understanding the problem

Spoke to local pharmacists and staff, users on medications to understand their current challenges and needs.

Ideation

Exploring directions

Narrowed down to key experiences, needs, the user flow when inquiring about traveling with medications.

Solution

This is My Med

Your travel guide with medications in one, first!

After adding your medications, the app will run through updated TSA regulations, and country regulations for you.

Takeaways

What I learned

The design process isn't always linear. Throughout there were many times when we would change features, real life constraints, and let go ideas.

Balancing the original design function and real world constraint took trial and error. But getting feedback from actual users and their personal experience was extremely helpful especially when it's for them.
