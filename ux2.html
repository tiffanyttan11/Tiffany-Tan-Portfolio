Project Title

A travel companion for people who manage medications — so the research is done before you ever reach the border.

12 weeks
Figma, Illustrator
Team of 3
UX Research, UI Design, Prototyping

Overview

Medication regulations vary by country, carrier, and border crossing — and there's no single place to find them.

Travelers check pharmacists, insurance providers, embassy websites, and Google — and still aren't sure they have the full picture. The information exists, it's just scattered everywhere.

We built the guide that doesn't exist yet.
